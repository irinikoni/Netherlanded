# City research – 10 study cities

*Researched 19 October 2026 for the Netherlanded app. Every fact below comes with a source link in `research/cities/*.json` and is stored in the database (`municipality`, `institution`, `step_local`, `service`).*

## How the app uses it

- **Where you live** decides registration, GP and housing info (`local_scope = 'home'`). If you live in a neighbouring municipality, the app tells you to register *there*, not in your study city, and that the study city's student registration days may not apply.
- **Where you study** decides residence-permit collection and intro week (`local_scope = 'study'`). Permit collection is arranged through your university, so it does not change when you live elsewhere; the IND receives your new address automatically after you register.
- If a neighbouring municipality has no housing or GP data, the app falls back to the study city's list and says so.

## Registration in each city

### Den Haag
New international students register at special sessions held at the start of each semester at The Hague International Centre (Spui 70). Appointments are booked online through the centre's student registration page. Students bring a passport or EU ID plus a copy, the residence permit or approval email, and a rental contract (or a declaration of permission from the main occupant).

*Booking:* For September 2026, appointments start 10 August 2026 via the THIC student registration booking page. Students arriving outside these sessions should phone the municipality.

Source: https://denhaag.nl/en/relocation-and-settling/registration-procedures-for-international-students.htm

### Amsterdam
International students first pre-register online with the City of Amsterdam (for Fall 2026 the window ran 13 July to 18 September 2026); universities do not do this for you. Once approved, you get an email to book an in-person slot on a student registration day (17 August to 25 September 2026) at Bos en Lommerplein 176, 1055 RW Amsterdam, where the BSN is issued quickly. Students who miss the pre-registration window book a regular first-registration appointment instead, which universities warn can delay the BSN by weeks.

*Booking:* Pre-register online, then book a student-registration-day slot via the email link; late arrivals call +31 20 624 1111 for a regular appointment, and HvA expects Spring 2027 pre-registration dates around December/January.

**Residence permit collection**
- University of Amsterdam: Non-EU students collect their residence permit only at the IND office in Amsterdam, by appointment booked at https://ind.nl/en/appointment-to-collect-document. The permit is usually ready about 3 weeks after biometrics, and you check its status in MyIND. UvA also runs centralised collection days in August/September.

Source: https://www.amsterdam.nl/en/civil-affairs/pre-registration-for-students/

### Rotterdam
Students start their first registration online on the Rotterdam municipality website, up to 4 weeks before arrival, and upload their ID and proof of address. They then get a case number and a link to book an in-person 'Studentenafspraak gemeente' (student appointment) at City Hall, Coolsingel 40, where the BSN is issued straight away. Addresses in postcode areas that need a housing permit (HVV) must register in person at the municipal office. EUR also mentions registration at campus One Stop Shops at the start of each semester.

*Booking:* After submitting the online form you receive a case number and booking link; choose the appointment type 'Studentenafspraak gemeente', because other types follow the regular procedure without an immediate BSN.

**Residence permit collection**
- Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences (Hogeschool Rotterdam): The Centre of International Affairs applies for the MVV/residence permit for you; non-EU students collect the permit from the IND in The Hague after arrival, and the university emails you when it is ready.

Source: https://www.rotterdam.nl/en/first-registration-in-the-netherlands/start-registration-in-the-netherlands-by-a-student

### Utrecht
International students staying more than 4 months must book an appointment to register within 5 days of arriving in the Netherlands. Registration takes place at the Utrecht International Center, and you book through its 'register as a new student' page. Bring your passport or ID, a rental contract (or the proprietor's signed address form) and, if applicable, your residence permit, MVV or proof of application; registration gives you your BSN.

*Booking:* Book an appointment via the Utrecht International Center's 'register as a new student' page within 5 days of arrival.

**Residence permit collection**
- HU University of Applied Sciences Utrecht: HU's International Office plans a residence-permit pick-up day at Utrecht Science Park, likely in early October, and emails students the date; permits not ready by then are collected at the IND office in Zwolle.

Source: https://www.utrecht.nl/city-of-utrecht/registration/registration-international-students

### Groningen
International students staying longer than 4 months must register in person with the municipality by appointment, booked online through the municipality's student registration page (RUG and Hanze both send students there). Visits to the Groningen Centrum counter (Kreupelstraat 1) are by appointment only. Bring a passport or ID card, a rental contract (or a permission form from the main resident), and, for non-EU students, the residence permit or IND proof/approval letter. The BSN is issued on the spot at the appointment.

*Booking:* Book an appointment online via the municipality's student registration page; no booking window or opening date is stated.

**Residence permit collection**
- University of Groningen: September starters who get an invitation can collect their residence permit card at the central IND student day in Utrecht (August/September). For February starters, the IND comes to the university in March to hand out cards. Anyone who misses these, or whose card isn't ready, collects it at the IND desk in Zwolle by appointment.

Source: https://gemeente.groningen.nl/en/student-registration-or-deregistration

### Leiden
Newcomers first fill in the online 'Register your move' form, then get an email with a link to book an in-person appointment at the Stadskantoor (Bargelaan 190), which should be within 5 days of moving. Leiden University says the municipality runs special registration sessions for new students at the start of each semester. Students bring a passport, the residence permit or Leiden University approval email, and a rental contract (or landlord permission plus a copy of the landlord's ID). The university says a birth certificate is generally not needed for its students, although the municipality's page lists one.

*Booking:* For September 2026, student-session appointments start from the third week of August 2026. Students arriving at other times should phone the town hall (14 071, or +31 71 51 65 165 from abroad).

Source: https://gemeente.leiden.nl/inwoners-en-ondernemers/verhuizen-en-migreren/migreren/moving-to-leiden-from-abroad/

### Delft
International students must register in person within 5 days of arrival, at an online-booked appointment at the Delft city office (Stationsplein 1). TU Delft says the appointment takes about 10 minutes. Students bring a passport, a printed rental contract (there is no printer on site), their student number and, for non-EU students, a copy of the MVV or the institution's residence-permit approval email.

*Booking:* Book online at https://www.delft.nl/registration. You can only book once your rental agreement has started.

Source: https://www.delft.nl/en/registration-international-students

### Eindhoven
Foreign students staying at least 4 of the coming 6 months must register in person at the municipality's Residents' Plaza (Stadhuisplein 1), preferably within 5 days of arrival, after first booking an appointment online (separate booking links for EU and non-EU students). Bring a valid passport, IND documents if applicable, original (legalised) foreign documents such as a birth certificate, and your rental contract; the BSN confirmation letter arrives within about 4 weeks. TU/e gives its students access to a booking portal with slots prioritized for TU/e students during the Bachelor Introduction Week or Master Kick-off, and some Fontys study departments arrange the appointment for their international students.

*Booking:* Book online before going (EU: https://eindhoven.mijnafspraakmaken.nl/client/#?product=6, non-EU: https://eindhoven.mijnafspraakmaken.nl/client/#?product=64) or by phone on 14 040; availability can be limited in peak periods.

Source: https://www.eindhoven.nl/stad-en-wonen/wonen/registration-of-foreign-students

### Maastricht
Maastricht University tells students moving from abroad to register their address with the municipality within five days to get a BSN. Residents of southern Limburg not yet in the BRP can book a combined BRP registration + BSN appointment at the Expat Centre Maastricht Region (in the Maastricht International Centre) by emailing expatcentre@maastricht.nl, though the IND page does not say this route is meant for students.

*Booking:* Booking details for regular student registration could not be confirmed; the Expat Centre combined appointment is requested via expatcentre@maastricht.nl.

Source: https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/study/student-life/coming-netherlands-abroad/registering-your-first-address-netherlands

### Enschede
International students staying longer than four months must register at Enschede city hall (Hengelosestraat 51) and get their BSN the same day. University of Twente students starting in February or September receive an email with a link to book their municipality appointment, and Saxion runs BSN registration days at Enschede city hall (weekdays 24 August-11 September 2026) where the International Office assigns each student a fixed time slot. Students arriving at other times book an individual appointment via the municipality's contact page.

*Booking:* UT students book via the link emailed before their intake; Saxion students get an emailed invitation with a fixed slot; others book via https://www.enschede.nl/en/english-contact-and-opening-hours.

**Residence permit collection**
- University of Twente: UT arranges group IND appointments: summer-intake pickups are normally at a central location and winter-intake pickups on campus; if you miss it, book an individual IND appointment and collect at the IND office in Zwolle.
- Saxion University of Applied Sciences: Collected at a central IND Student Day at Jaarbeurs Utrecht (Beatrix Building) on Saturday 29 August or Saturday 19 September 2026; the date is assigned by email about two weeks ahead and booked via the emailed link.

Source: https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/student-services/visa/appointment-formalities.rtd/register-municipality/
