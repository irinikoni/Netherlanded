The laws involved

Two questions: may the app read and write on the phone, and may any data leave it?

Dutch cookie rule

Telecommunicatiewet 11.7a

Storage on the phone needs consent unless strictly necessary or analytics with little privacy impact by the service provider. Netherlanded is that provider, so Tier 1 may run without consent while it measures the app's own quality.

EU privacy law

GDPR / AVG

Tier 1: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) with the right to object shown clearly (Art. 21(4)). Tier 2: specific opt-in consent (Art. 6(1)(a), 7) naming both purposes.

Dutch implementation

UAVG, age 16

Terms require 16+. Students aged 16–17 are minors, which weighs heavily in every legitimate-interest balance.

Consumer & cyber law

Civil Code & NIS2 (Cbw)

Consumer terms keep mandatory rights. No student identifiers on servers keeps vendor risk low for universities.
