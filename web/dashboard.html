Prototype with fictional data. In the live service, each university sees only its own institution-scoped snapshot (floor ≥ 15, rounded to 5, tail-capped ≤10% / ≥90%).
How these figures protect students
  • Students control consent separately across Tier 1 (coarse service totals) and Tier 2 (optional anonymised bottleneck totals), and can switch either off at any time.
  • No name, device ID, IP address, or exact arrival date is ever stored. Cohorts are grouped by intake semester (YYYY-sep / YYYY-feb).
  • Every group under 15 students is withheld, counts under 50 are rounded to 5, rates are tail-capped at ≤ 10.0% / ≥ 90.0%, and complementary + sticky suppression block small-change inference across releases.
  • Raw event buffers fold hourly and auto-purge cleanly under the 48-hour ceiling; universities read only pre-built scheduled snapshots.
